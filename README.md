## About me

I'm interested in the full journey of an AI system: understanding the problem, preparing the data, choosing a model, and making the result useful to someone.

- **Building:** LLM-powered applications, RAG workflows, and ML pipelines with real-world deployment in mind.
- **Exploring:** agentic workflows, advanced LLM orchestration, reinforcement learning integration, and efficient model deployment.
- **Looking to collaborate on:** AI products, NLP applications, recommendation systems, and thoughtful model evaluation.
- **Always happy to discuss:** document retrieval, transformer-based text analysis, YOLO detection, and the tradeoffs behind an applied AI pipeline.

<details>
<summary><b>What connects my projects?</b></summary>

I like working where different parts of AI meet: turning a text emotion prediction into synthesized speech, connecting object detection to product recommendations, or combining several text classifiers into a research pipeline.

I'm also interested in what happens beyond the model: data quality, evaluation, readable documentation, and the limitations that affect how a result should be used.

</details>
